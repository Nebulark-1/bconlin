    The plan

    Life is beautiful, and there's so much of it left.

    I think most of the good stuff is small. Snow piling up outside the window. A long run that ends at the lake. An hour gone, just watching the fish drift around their tank. The more I slow down and notice it, the more of it there seems to be.

    So I follow what feels alive. I moved to the Upper Peninsula knowing no one, and fell for the winters, the saunas, and the ice baths in Lake Superior. I left an office for a Level I trauma center. I finished an Ironman 70.3, and once freedived 80 feet just to chase a rock that looked cool. Every one of those started with saying yes.

    Wherever I go next, I hope it feels the same. Somewhere I'm still learning, still curious, and still a little amazed by how good an ordinary day can be.
